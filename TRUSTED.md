## Trusted base (from the design spec)

- rustc, Verus (including its `by (compute)` interpreter), Z3, and vstd's specs of std.
- The OS UDP stack, the clock, and getrandom (used for connection IDs, stateless-reset tokens, and each TLS handshake's `TLS_RAND_LEN` bytes: the ServerHello random, the ECDHE scalar and the PSS salt).
- The unverified IO shell and the `main` binary, including loading the certificate chain and parsing the PEM/PKCS#8 P-256 private key and, when configured, the PKCS#8 RSA private key.
- **The hand-written Verus formalization of each RFC** (`spec` modules). Proofs show the code matches these specs; they can't show the specs match the RFC text. Keeping the specs small and readable, with each one citing its RFC section, is how the risk stays manageable.
- vstd models a `Vec`'s length, not its capacity, so every proven memory bound (e.g. `quic::reassembly`'s `phys_len() <= 2 * buf_len()` and `RecvStream`'s `buffered_phys()`) bounds lengths only. `Reassembly::pop` releases both allocations on a full drain by construction (fresh `Vec`s, and `split_off` keeps only the tail allocation); the unverified test-only accessor `Reassembly::capacities` checks it in `tests/reassembly.rs` (`drain_releases_capacity`); likewise `SendStream::on_data_acked` replaces its buffer with a fresh `Vec` once everything written is acknowledged, checked through `SendStream::buf_capacity` in `tests/send_stream.rs` (`ack_releases_capacity`; `ack_copies_linear` uses it to count the bytes its lazy trims copy); and `RecvPns`'s range list holds at most `MAX_ACK_RANGES` + 1 entries at any moment (the lowest is popped in place, never re-allocated), checked through `RecvPns::ranges_capacity` in `tests/ack_track.rs` (`ranges_capacity_bounded`); and `SentPackets::on_ack` and `drain_inflight` leave a fresh `Vec` (the rebuild and the swap), checked through `SentPackets::inflight_capacity` in `tests/sent.rs` (`ack_frees`, `drain_frees`). `SentPackets` itself derives nothing (its ghost `issued` set has no `Debug`). `PeerCids`'s retire lists hold at most `2 * PEER_CID_CAP` entries together (proven in its `wf`), and their capacity is checked through `PeerCids::retire_capacity` in `tests/cid.rs` (`retire_backlog`); its active list holds at most `PEER_CID_CAP` entries (proven), and the Retire Prior To split keeps the tail as a fresh `Vec` (capacity not tested); `PeerCids` derives nothing either (its ghost `received` map and `acked` set have no `Debug`). `CidTable::remove_conn` rebuilds both of its lists as fresh `Vec`s (each at most `MAX_TABLE` entries, proven in its `wf`), checked through `CidTable::capacities` in `tests/route.rs` (`remove_conn_frees`). `AmpBudget` derives nothing (its ghost trace has no `Debug`), nor do `Path` and `Paths` (they hold one). `Paths` holds at most `MAX_PATHS` paths and each path at most `MAX_RESPONSES` owed responses (proven in its `wf`; the oldest is removed in place), checked through `Paths::capacities` in `tests/path.rs` (`capacity_bounded`).
- **Temporary:** each crypto `external_body` backed by the RustCrypto shim (phases 3-4). Phase 5 removes every one of these entries.

## Pinned versions

- Verus 0.2026.10.04.426d8b0 (toolchain 1.98.1)
- Z3 4.16.0 (bundled with Verus)
- vstd 0.0.0-2026-10-04-0306 (must equal the pin in vest_lib 0.2.2)

The shipped artifact is built by plain `cargo build` on the user's default stable rustc (unpinned; currently 1.92.0), with `verus!` erasure done by `verus_builtin_macros` (version from `Cargo.lock`, currently 0.0.0-2026-09-20-0158). Verification used Verus's own rustc 1.98.1 frontend, so the compiler that checks the proofs is not the compiler that builds the binary. No `rust-toolchain.toml` is needed.

- Test-only (crates/tls dev-dependencies, not in the trusted base; `cargo tree -e normal` contains none of them). These are the Cargo.lock entries this phase adds:
  - ring =0.17.14, with untrusted 0.9.0 and the getrandom 0.2.17 *crate*, which is test-only and distinct from the trusted OS getrandom above;
  - ring's build/platform entries: cc 1.6.0, shlex 2.0.1, find-msvc-tools 0.1.14, wasi 0.11.1+wasi-snapshot-preview1, windows-sys 0.52.0, windows-targets 0.52.6 and windows_* 0.52.6;
  - rustls =0.23.45 (Task 7), with rustls-webpki 0.103.15, rustls-pki-types 1.15.1 and once_cell 1.21.4. Its subtle 2.6.1 and zeroize 1.9.1 were already locked by the RustCrypto shim.
- RustCrypto shim (crates/crypto), removed in phase 5: sha2 =0.11.1, p256 =0.14.0, rsa =0.10.0-rc.19 (dev: primeorder =0.14.0, rsa with hazmat, hmac =0.13.0, hkdf =0.13.0, aes =0.9.3, aes-gcm =0.11.1); transitively pinned by Cargo.lock: hmac 0.13.0, hkdf 0.13.0, rfc6979 0.6.0, ecdsa 0.17.0, crypto-bigint 0.7.10, primeorder 0.14.0 (a normal dependency), elliptic-curve 0.14.3, digest 0.11.3
